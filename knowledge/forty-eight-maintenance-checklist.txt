Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight Maintenance
Checklist (Kilometers)
Interval (km / time)
Task
Every ride
Check tire pressure & condition, oil level, brake fluid, lights, belt tension, and leaks.
Every 8,000 km / 1 year
Change engine oil & filter; inspect brakes; check throttle/clutch free play; clean/replace air filte
Every 16,000 km
Change primary chaincase fluid; inspect spark plugs.
Every 32,000 km
Change transmission fluid; replace spark plugs; change fork oil.
Every 2 years
Replace brake fluid.
Every 3–5 years
Replace battery if weak; replace tires if 5–7 years old regardless of tread.
