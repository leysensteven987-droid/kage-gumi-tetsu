Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight Quick Maintenance
Checklist (Kilometers)
Interval (km / time)
Checklist Items
Before every ride
 Tire pressure & condition
 Oil level
 Brake fluid level
 Lights & signals
 Drive belt tension
 Look for leaks
Every 8,000 km / 1 year
 Change engine oil & filter
 Inspect brakes
 Check throttle & clutch free play
 Clean/replace air filter
Every 16,000 km
 Change primary chaincase fluid
 Inspect spark plugs
Every 32,000 km
 Change transmission fluid
 Replace spark plugs
 Change fork oil
Every 2 years
 Replace brake fluid
Every 3–5 years
 Replace battery if weak
 Replace tires if 5–7 years old
Winter storage
 Fill tank + add stabilizer
 Put battery on tender
 Cover with breathable motorcycle cover
 Elevate tires if long storage
