Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight Full Maintenance
Guide (Kilometers)
I Battery Care
- Ride every 1–2 weeks to keep the battery charged.
- Use a battery tender when parked long-term.
- Check terminals for corrosion and tightness.
- Replace every 3–5 years if weak (≈ 50,000 km of use).
I Fuel System
- Use premium fuel (high-octane).
- For storage longer than 1–2 months, add fuel stabilizer.
- Run engine briefly to circulate stabilizer into fuel lines.
II Engine Oil & Filter
- Change every 8,000 km or once a year.
- Change oil before winter storage.
- Use Harley-approved 20W-50 oil (synthetic or mineral depending on climate).
II Primary & Transmission Fluids
- Primary chaincase fluid: change every 16,000 km.
- Transmission fluid: change every 32,000 km.
- Inspect regularly for leaks.
II Drive Belt
- Inspect for cracks/wear every 4,000 km.
- Check belt tension and adjust if necessary.
- Keep pulleys clean, avoid harsh cleaners.
I Brakes
- Inspect pads and rotors every 8,000 km.
- Replace pads when thickness < 3 mm.
- Replace brake fluid every 2 years.
- Check for leaks and ensure firm lever/pedal feel.
I Tires
- Check pressure before every ride (≈ 2.5 bar front / 2.75 bar rear).
- Inspect for cracks, flat spots, or uneven wear.
- Replace at 5–7 years regardless of tread condition.
II Controls & Cables
- Check throttle and clutch free play every 8,000 km.
- Lubricate if stiff or sticky.
- Inspect for fraying or stiffness.
I Suspension
- Inspect fork seals for leaks.
- Adjust rear shock preload when carrying a passenger or luggage.
- Change fork oil every 32,000 km or sooner with heavy riding.
I Spark Plugs & Air Filter
- Inspect spark plugs every 16,000 km.
- Replace spark plugs every 32,000 km.
- Clean/replace air filter every 8,000 km (more often in dusty conditions).
I Electrical System
- Test headlights, signals, brake lights, and horn regularly.
- Keep spare fuses in your toolkit.
II General Pre-Ride Checks
- Tire pressure & condition.
- Oil level.
- Brake fluid level.
- Lights and signals.
- Belt tension.
- Leaks under the bike.
I Cleaning & Storage
- Wash with mild soap, avoid high-pressure water near electrics.
- Dry and wax chrome/paint to prevent rust.
- Winter storage:
- • Fill tank + fuel stabilizer.
- • Keep battery on tender.
- • Cover with breathable motorcycle cover.
- • Slightly elevate tires for very long storage.
