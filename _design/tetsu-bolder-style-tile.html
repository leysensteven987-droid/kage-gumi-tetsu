DIRECTION 02 Black Chrome

The showroom at midnight, lights on the tank. The current identity taken to eleven: true black ground, chrome re-polished from brushed to specular — near-white highlights, deeper wells — and the type scale doubled. Brighter means more light IN the metal, not more colour. The odometer stops being a widget and becomes the hero numeral. Status gets one glow: red, only when the bike is asking for the wrench.

DIRECTION 03 Weld & Stencil

The fabrication bay, not the showroom. "Rawer" taken literally: diamond-plate steel ground, torch-cut corners instead of border-radius, weld-stitch seams, ruler ticks, paint-pen outlined numerals and stencilled status tags slapped on at a slight angle. Statuses are painted plates — brass, chalk, and a rough red DUE stamp. The most aggressive of the three; every card reads like a job ticket zip-tied to the frame.

Recommendation

02 · Black Chrome is the recommended direction. It is the current locked identity with the volume turned to eleven — nothing is lost, everything hits harder: hotter specular chrome, a doubled type scale, the odometer promoted from widget to hero numeral, and one earned glow (red, only on DUE). It is also the closest match to the H-D.com reference's night-mode energy while keeping the dark-native character the app already has.

01 · Forge Light is the boldest read of "brighter" — and the closest to what H-D.com actually looks like in daylight (white ground, giant black type). If day-garage legibility matters more than midnight mood, pick this; it also inverts cleanly (see the day/night strip). 03 · Weld & Stencil is the rawest — torch-cut corners and painted stamps — best mined for accents (the OVERDUE stamp, ruler ticks, weld seams) even if 01 or 02 wins.
